# 🧟 Zombiverse — NASA Space Apps Challenge

An interactive C++ survival game built for the NASA Space Apps Challenge Hackathon.

## 🚀 About the Project

Zombiverse tests your survival decision-making across five celestial environments — Sun, Earth, Moon, Mars, and Jupiter. Players choose their environment and face 10 scenario-based questions to determine if they'd survive a zombie apocalypse in space.

## 🎮 How to Play

- Sign up and log in — incorrect credentials end the game
- Choose one of 2 stars or 3 planets
- Start with 5 life points
- Answer 10 questions about your chosen celestial body
- Wrong answers reduce life by 1
- Lucky charms appear after questions 3 and 7 — if the charm shows number 5, gain 1 life
- Survive all questions to win!

- Developed solo under hackathon conditions
